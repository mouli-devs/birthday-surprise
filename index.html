A Letter For You

Happy birthday, Princy ❤️🥹

It's hard to believe how many years have already gone by since we became best friends! We literally grew up together and somehow managed to survive all the stupid fights, tears, laughs, conversations... and u somehow remained one of the people who were closest to me all these years.

When u moved to the USA, I thought that something would change for us since having u half the world away is barely a deal for someone like me 😭, but somehow mana bond lo em change raledu. We still manage to talk to each other every single day, send each other ridiculous reels, discuss every possible topic that comes to mind, argue about dumb things, and then come back like nothing happened 😂.

I think that's what I love the most about u, Princy.

Entha distance unna, enni years ayina, u eppudu same Princy lagaane untav. ❤️

Being the person who somehow grew up with me and saw me growing up, knowing me at almost every stage of my life — na childhood nunchi ippati varaku nannu chusina person uve. I honestly can't imagine a bigger honour than that.

Some people come into ur life and eventually become just a memory, while some people stay and become a part of ur story. Nuvvu na story lo oka important part ayipoyav, and I don't think that's something that can just disappear because of distance. 😊

Mana friendship lo naaku nachina biggest thing enti ante, manam entha maarina, mana madhya unna comfort matram maaraledu. We can talk about literally anything, be completely stupid together, argue over the smallest things, and then continue talking like nothing happened 😭😂.

I'm grateful for every single conversation, every stupid message, every inside joke, every random chat, ur laughter that somehow knew how to find its way to me when I needed it most, and even ur anger and annoyance that somehow made me feel awful 😭.

U kopam lo unna, happy ga unna, edustunna, navvutunna — life lo em jarugutunna, somehow natho share cheskune person u. And honestly, adi naaku chala special. ❤️

But no matter how far u are, I sincerely hope that u know there's always someone here who cares for u, supports u, listens to u no matter what and no matter how long the story is, who'll be happy to see u 😂, and who'll obviously keep bothering u with questions about ur travels.

U eppudu gurthupettuko — neeku em jarigina, entha dooram unna, ikkada nenu unna.

Ur friend, ur personal reel sender, ur travel-question machine, and unfortunately, someone who's never going to stop annoying u 😭😂❤️.

I hope ur New Year brings u all the possible happiness and wonderful things and maybe the 10th BOY kuda into ur world, Princy! 😭😂

I hope u do beautiful things, make beautiful plans, meet beautiful people, achieve beautiful goals! I hope u experience everything u dream of, have a fantastic time, be always happy and do all the things u like most in this world!

Nee life lo u korukunna prati okkati jaragali. U eppudu happy ga undali, peaceful ga undali, and ur face meeda aa smile eppudu undali. ❤️

And selfishly, aa beautiful moments anni nenu kuda choodali, vini enjoy cheyyali, and somehow vaatilo oka small part aina undali ani korukuntunna. ❤️

Happy birthday to my friend, Princy! 🫶🏻

Thank u for growing up with me and somehow managing to be my friend despite so many years we spent together 😭😂.

Mana childhood nunchi ippati varaku vachina ee journey gurinchi alochiste, chala memories gurthostayi. Konni navvinchevi, konni emotional chesevi, konni "manam asalu ila ela friends ayyamu ra?" ani anipinchevi 😂 — but every single one of them is special because u were a part of them.

Enni years ayina, entha distance vachina, life manalni ekkadiki teesukellina... okati matram nijam — manam okariki okaram strangers aipokudadhu. ❤️

Have fun at ur birthday, birthday girl! 🥳❤️
Enjoy ur day, eat something nice, make some memories, and nannu marchipokunda konchem na gurinchi kuda think cheyyi 😭😂.

And don't let this message deceive u because nuvvu ippatiki kuda anthe annoying ga unnav, em change avvaledu 😭😂❤️.

Happy birthday once again, Princy. ❤️
Chaala happy ga undu, always. 🫶🏻
